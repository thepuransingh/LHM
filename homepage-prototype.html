Our Story

About

Lovewave Harmony Music creates original Soul-Motown ballads about destiny, devotion, and unfinished love. With warm Rhodes piano, cinematic strings, and a slow Motown pulse, each song is made for couples, late-night listeners, and old-school R&B hearts. Start with When Beauty Stole My Heart — our most-loved song.

Hi, we're Lovewave Harmony Music.

We write and produce original soft, heartfelt love songs inspired by 70s Soul, Motown, and old-school R&B slow jams.

No covers. No noise. Just honest songs about the moments love leaves behind — Where Our Dreams Become One, Something In You Feels Like Home, When Beauty Stole My Heart.

Every track is an original composition, produced with modern tools including AI-assisted arrangement, then human-curated for emotion, story, and soul.

Press play. Stay for the quiet part. That's where the love lives.
